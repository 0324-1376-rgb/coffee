About Us

A small cafe with a simple goal: a good cup and a good pastry, every day.

About the coffee shop

Daybreak Coffee Corner is a neighborhood cafe where students, families, and workers stop by for a quiet seat and a warm drink. Our menu is short on purpose, so we can make every item well.

Our story

Daybreak started in 2022 as a tiny coffee cart outside a local school, serving early-morning commuters. People kept coming back, so we opened our own corner shop in 2024. We still wake up before sunrise to brew the first pot and bake the first batch.

Our mission and values

Our mission is to make everyday moments better with honest coffee and fresh pastries.

  • Quality firstGood ingredients, brewed and baked with care.
  • Freshness dailyPastries are baked the same day they are sold.
  • CommunityA welcoming space for everyone in the neighborhood.
  • Fair pricesAffordable treats you can enjoy every day.
